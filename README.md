# Ramani Desk

A workspace for architects in Tanzania, built from a real architect interview.

- **Brief & design:** type the client's brief ("4 bedrooms, master ensuite, 2 floors, plot 20x30") and get a concept plan with measurements and a room schedule.
- **Start from a drawing:** upload a photo of a sketch or an image/PDF plan, set the scale with one known length, outline the rooms, and get an editable 2D plan and 3D model.
- **2D, elevations and 3D:** real walls with doors and windows (placed automatically or by hand), L-shaped rooms, gable, hip or flat roofs, front/back/side elevations with levels, and a 3D model on its plot with sun and shadow.
- **Drawings out:** A3 approval sheets at 1:100 with title block, room and door/window schedules (print or save as PDF); DXF export for Archicad, AutoCAD and SketchUp.
- **Drawings in:** photo, image, PDF or DXF (rooms detected automatically from DXF).
- **Materials & cost:** rough material list and building cost using local market prices, plus a summary to send the client.
- **Approval check:** flags common reasons Lands rejects drawings: wastewater, floors, land use, room height, windows, coverage, setbacks.
- **Fees & payments:** tracks the project stages, deposit and balance, and charges for revisions beyond the free ones.

Works offline once opened, and can be installed on a phone (browser menu → Add to Home Screen). Projects save on the device; use **Back up** to save a copy and **Restore** to bring it back.

Estimates and checks are a first pass for early decisions. Final drawings, structural design and bills of quantities still come from the engineer and quantity surveyor.
